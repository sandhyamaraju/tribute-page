About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs.

He was known for his dedication, simplicity and inspiring attitude towards students and young people.

Achievements

  • Worked with ISRO and DRDO.
  • Played a key role in India's missile programs.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna.
  • Inspired millions of students across India.
